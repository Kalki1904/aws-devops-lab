Our Story

Knotted & Loved was born in a small apartment in Bangalore, with just two hooks, a ball of cotton yarn, and an abundance of passion. What started as a hobby became a platform that celebrates the timeless art of crochet.

We connect talented makers with people who appreciate handmade beauty. Every item in our store is crafted by real humans, with real care — never mass-produced.
